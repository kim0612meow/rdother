# Lowered project voice recordings

Owner-provided recordings, authorized for this game. Rights remain with the owner; **not CC0**. See [original recording credits](../audio-v41/CREDITS.md).

- hey-stop.mp3: victim detection / face recognition.
- what-are-you-doing.mp3: bystander detection.

Derived from the preserved audio-v41 recordings on 2026-10-26. Pitch lowered 3 semitones using Rubber Band with formants preserved and tempo unchanged. Gentle 85 Hz high-pass and 4500 Hz low-pass remove rumble and soften sharp high frequencies. Game playback gain reduced from 0.65 to 0.40 (about 38% lower amplitude). No artificial reverb or playback-speed changes. Original recordings remain available in audio-v41. Processing provenance and hashes are in SOURCES.json.
