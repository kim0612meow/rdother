- **hey-stop.mp3**: “Hey, stop!” — from owner-supplied 2026-10-26 17-57-20.mp4; plays when the victim detects theft, or recognizes the player's face later.

Source videos remain outside the repository and are not published. Segment boundaries and processed-file SHA-256 hashes are in SOURCES.json.
