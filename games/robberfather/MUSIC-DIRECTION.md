# Background music direction

User direction, 2026-10-25: The protagonist is an ordinary impoverished father sleeping on the street with his son and stealing to feed him. He is neither a hero nor a celebrated villain. The city is indifferent and offers no rescue; the intended story has only bad endings.

Music selection should support exhaustion, uncertainty and cold everyday life. Seek sparse, subdued arrangements with low-register weight and little sense of uplift. Keep space for footsteps and other ordinary sounds. Shop music should retain this emotional world. Avoid bright upper-register piano, comforting relaxation music, medieval/tavern music, comic stealth, heroic orchestration, and horror spectacle. These are music selection criteria, not instructions to change gameplay, existing story, artwork or UI.

Rejected audition sets:
- Hidden Agenda; Medieval: Market Day; Medieval: The Old Tower Inn; Medieval: Exploration.
- Sunset Walk; Relaxing Piano Music; Calm Piano 1 (Vaporware); Dream Culture.

Current auditions are in music-review.html. They are original external streams, not installed gameplay tracks. Descriptions are based on the composers' published classifications; playback checks do not establish subjective fit. Do not describe them as listened-to or approved without evidence.
